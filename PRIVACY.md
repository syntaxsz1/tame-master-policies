# Tame Master — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Tame Master is a Discord bot game ("the Bot") where players hunt, tame, and collect virtual monsters, build coin-earning plots, and battle, raid, and trade with other players. This policy explains what data the Bot stores and why.

## What we store

The Bot stores only the data it needs to run the game:

- **Discord user ID** — to link your game progress to your account.
- **Discord server (guild) ID and channel ID** — to remember server settings, such as which channel wild monsters spawn in.
- **Game progress** — your coins, the monsters you have tamed (name, rarity, level, stats), your monster index ("dex") progress, your plot and which monsters are placed on it, items you own (such as lures and shields), upgrades, prestige level, battle wins and losses, catch and daily streaks, clan membership, season earnings, and cooldown timestamps.

Your game progress is shared across every server where you play with the Bot — your collection and coins follow your account, not a single server.

## What we do NOT store

- We do **not** read or store the content of your messages. The Bot works through slash commands and buttons only.
- We do **not** collect your name, email address, password, payment information, or location.
- We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** share it with advertisers or data brokers.

## How the data is used

Your data is used only to operate the game: saving your progress between sessions, showing your collection, plot, and profile, running leaderboards, battles, raids, and trades, and preventing cheating or abuse of game features.

Some game information is visible to other players by design — for example, leaderboards show player display names and scores, and your plot and profile can be viewed by other players in the server.

## Where the data is kept

Game data is stored in a database on our hosting provider's servers (Fly.io). We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

## Data removal

You can ask for your game data to be deleted at any time. Contact us through the official Tame Master community Discord server, and we will delete your saved game progress (coins, monsters, plot, and related records) from the Bot's database. Deleting your data cannot be undone.

## Children

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age required by Discord in their country.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: reach the developer through the official **Tame Master** community Discord server.
